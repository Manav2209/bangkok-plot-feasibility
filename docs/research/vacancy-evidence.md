# Vacancy evidence remains unresolved

Research date: 22 November 2026. No current vacant-site finding was established.

The default outline is a geometry-only translation inside BMA's sourced 2013 zone. That
establishes membership in the mapped polygon, not title, parcel boundaries, current legal
overlays or vacancy. Existing mapped buildings remain visible in the app.

Bounded acquisition routes investigated:

- BMA imagery/basemap service metadata: a service edition/year does not prove an imagery
  acquisition date. Metadata snapshots are retained with the other GIS research files.
- OpenAerialMap: the exact supported-zone bounding-box search returned no coverage during the
  research attempt. No aerial vacancy observation was obtained.
- KartaView's public photo endpoint around the illustrative site returned dated street-photo
  metadata, mainly from 2019/2020 in the inspected initial result set. A photo's shot date is
  distinct from upload/retrieval dates; no inspected photo established current vacancy.
- OpenStreetMap footprints: missing buildings cannot establish vacancy, and a footprint's
  mapping timestamp is not a current land-condition survey.

A completed acceptance record needs an attributable image/observation date, a visible vacant
site, its position and extent matched to the zone, a separately labelled illustrative/cadastral
boundary and relevant road/overlay evidence. A historic observation must remain explicitly
historic. Do not convert the available metadata or absence of mapped buildings into a pass.
