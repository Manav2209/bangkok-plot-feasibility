# Bounded amendment and parking-ramp audit

Research date: 23 November 2026. Scope: MR33/50/69, MR55 and Bangkok2544 parking/access.
This is a finite source-by-rule audit, not a representation that every amendment through
this date has been found. No application evidence status is promoted by this document.
The earlier numerical research remains in `legal-evidence-04.md`.

## Source-by-rule applicability matrix

Each entry distinguishes located text from completeness and actual project applicability.

1. **MR33 access/classification; MR50 replacement wording; MR69 emergency access.**
   The [official OCS index and public download](https://www.ocs.go.th/searchlaw/law-index/item/13926)
   supplied the 38-page original/amendment bundle inspected in memo04. It positively establishes
   those instruments, including MR69's Gazette4June2021 publication. The bundle is not an
   integrated current-law certificate. This pass did not retrieve a post-2021 replacement
   of their access clauses from OCS, Gazette or BMA. That is an unresolved audit result,
   **not proof of absence**. Keep current-completeness and parcel-application gates unknown.

2. **MR55 road, waterway, height and boundary controls.** A more complete
   [OCS consolidated text hosted by official OIC](https://www.oic.go.th/FILEWEB/CABINFOCENTER71/DRAWER055/GENERAL/DATA0000/00000019.PDF)
   was located. Footnotes identify MR58, MR61, MR66 and MR68. This improves the earlier
   source that only showed58/61; it does not establish that no post-2020 amendment exists.
   Use the consolidation as a traceable located text, subject to later-law verification.

3. **MR66 and skywalks.** [Original Gazette,19August2016](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/2079860.pdf)
   adds MR55's inter-building walkway definition and clauses32/1–32/3. Qualifying walkway
   area and underlying open-space treatment have conditions. No skywalk is established for
   the illustrative site, so do not grant an area exclusion or road-cover exception by default.

4. **MR68 and MR55 definitions/height/exceptions.**
   [BMA's official forwarding letter and Gazette copy](https://infocenter.oic.go.th/FILEWEB/CABINFOCENTER9/DRAWER048/GENERAL/DATA0000/00000951.PDF)
   identifies publication3December2020. It adds building-line and blank-wall definitions;
   adds a clause44 paragraph treating qualifying other structures on a roof separately;
   adds clauses51–53 and transitional54. Building-line means outermost wall, column or
   stair, except an open external fire-escape stair. Clause51 concerns specified tanks,
   antennas and other tall structures; setback is `min(6,max(3,height/8))` metres when
   its classification triggers. Clauses52/53 give tightly conditioned pre-existing row-building
   relief; they are not ordinary new-condominium exemptions. Relevant classification,
   pre-existing permission, subdivision date and rooftop object status remain unknown.
   OCR of the reproduced Gazette headers is inconsistent; the date is corroborated by
   the OCS consolidation. Inspect rendered originals before entering an exact volume number.

## Parking-ramp constraints ready for reference implementation

[Bangkok2544](https://webportal.bangkok.go.th/public/user_files_editor/76/law/tk24.pdf),
clauses92,99–100, printed29–30:

- Parking-building scope: ≥10spaces or indoor parking/aisles/turning area≥300m².
- Straight interstorey ramp slope≤15%; flight rise≤5m; taller ramps need rest length≥6m.
- Curved/spiral ramp inner-edge radius≥6m; slope≤12%.
- Ground-level ramp start≥6m from building/vehicle entrance or exit mouth.
- Parking surface slope≤5%.

Reference arithmetic: `gradePercent=100*abs(rise)/horizontalRun`; inclined straight run≥`rise/0.15`;
`flightCount=ceil(rise/5)`; intermediate rests=`max(0,flightCount-1)`, each≥6m.
These give minimum schematic lengths, excluding vertical-transition design and manoeuvring.
Do not automatically overlap a rest with the ground approach.

Fixtures:15% passes/15.001% fails; curved12% passes/12.001% fails; inner radius6m passes;
rise5m needs no intermediate rest,5.001m needs one; parking5% passes/5.001% fails.
Zero horizontal run with positive rise is invalid. Preserve missing rise/run as unknown.

## Headroom, turning and transitions

[Official BMA-hosted local-official building-control manual](https://webportal.bangkok.go.th/public/user_files_editor/426/Manual/%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B9%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%88%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%99%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%B5%E0%B9%88.pdf),
printed145–146, explicitly attributes:

- MR41clause4: net vertical clearance over stalls, aisles and ramps≥2.10m to the lowest
  part of the next storey. A limited staggered-parking overlap≤1m exception exists;
  do not automate it without the original geometry and instrument.
- MR41clause3: stall markings and direct connection to circulation/entry/turning area.
- MR7clause7: suitably located, adequately sized turning area and marked turning paths;
  continuous one-way entry-to-exit has an exception.
- MR41clause2: perpendicular parking includes a restriction against one-way vehicle access.

These are positively located agency guidance with exact instrument references; the original
MR41 Gazette and complete amendment chain were not retrieved in this pass. Do not infer
a universal car turning radius from the6m inner radius of a **curved ramp**. Do not apply
ordinary2.10m headroom to fire access or ambulance space, which have distinct controls.

No exact statutory vertical transition-curve length, crest/sag radius, vehicle wheelbase,
overhang, swept-path envelope or universal car turning radius was established from the
inspected primary sources. Any adopted design vehicle and transition reserve must be
labelled an engineering assumption, with actual clearance/swept-path checks still pending.

## Audit routes and stopping condition

Inspected official OCS bundle; OIC consolidated MR55; BMA ordinance,2025 public-works
manual and current Public Works legal directory; original Gazette MR66/MR71; official
Gazette copies MR68/MR72 and BMA2023 repeal. MR71 and72 were rendered visually because
text extraction is corrupted. Searches targeted exact instrument numbers and BE2565–2569
on OCS, Gazette, BMA, DPT and the central law portal. No absence conclusion is drawn from
unindexed results, timeouts, inaccessible attachments or a current webpage's display date.

The [current BMA Public Works legal directory](https://webportal.bangkok.go.th/public/yota/page/sub/24690)
lists MR71 and2023/2024 instruments; its limited list is a discovery aid, not an exhaustive
change log. Official-source research did not establish a complete current supersession
chain for each target family. That is the precise remaining legal-audit deliverable.

Implementation can now add explicit ramp, rest, headroom and turning-evidence fields and
numeric reference checks. A concept still requires the actual parking section, vehicle
route, structure/services clearance, public-road access and current applicability review
before any overall parking or legal pass is justified.

Only this new research document was changed; no code, ledger or legal profile was promoted.
