## FAR incentives and parking counting

The [BMA-hosted 2013 planning text](https://webportal.bangkok.go.th/public/user_files_editor/76/law/y10.pdf),
printed pages 77–79, clauses 51–57, contains conditional FAR incentives for specified housing,
public parking, water retention and energy performance, with a combined increase capped at 20%.
Qualifying project evidence is required; ordinary private parking is not automatically exempt.

Clause 53's specified public-open-space scheme lists Y.8–Y.10 and P.2–P.5, rather than Y.7.
Clause 54's named-station public-parking scheme excludes its qualifying additional public parking
from FAR/OSR counting. Bang Chak is not in that named station list. Neither provision establishes
a general parking deduction for the illustrative example. No incentive bonus is claimed or applied.

The app now explicitly explains that its study uses base reference ratios and geometric gross
area, without a FAR incentive or parking-area deduction. Regulated area remains unknown. This
clarifies model scope; it is not a substitute for complete counting definitions and amendments.

## Keep denominator provenance separate

[BMA building ordinance 2544](https://webportal.bangkok.go.th/public/user_files_editor/76/law/tk24.pdf),
clause 52, printed page 15, uses plot area for the cited residential/nonresidential uncovered-space
percentages. The [BMA-hosted MR55 consolidation through MR68](https://webportal.bangkok.go.th/public/user_files_editor/426/mr43-55-upd68.pdf),
clause 33, PDF page 7, uses the largest floor area for its own 30%/10% provision. These are different
instrument-specific conditions. The existing BMA-based plot-area study target must not be changed
to the national denominator merely because the percentages resemble each other.

The consolidation identifies amendments 58, 61, 66 and 68. It corroborates the previously located
consolidation; it does not establish that there has been no later amendment. Current completeness,
project applicability, classification and other overlays remain separate gates.

## Zone-data terms and accuracy

The exact [LUP_POLYGON catalog](https://data.go.th/en/dataset/lup_polygon) continues to display
“License not specified”. Its footer links [DGA Open Government License terms](https://data.go.th/th/pages/dga-open-government-license).
The [English v1.0 terms dated 10 June 2021](https://data.go.th/uploads/page_images/2021-06-11-053225.908526Terms-and-Conditions-for-DGA-Open-Government-LicenseEN04062021.pdf),
PDF pages 3–4, permit copying, publication, distribution and research use of platform data with
source/producer attribution and no misleading alteration. They exclude intellectual property DGA
has no authority to license and include warranty limitations.

**Inference:** this gives a plausible general portal reuse basis for catalogued data. It does not
replace the dataset-specific unspecified label, prove third-party rights clearance or establish
explicit coverage for the independently served GIS extract bundled here. No CC/ODbL zone licence
is invented. Attribution remains in the map and manifest; the general terms and their limited
evidential scope are now recorded in the manifest and source drawer.

The catalog's description and four extent coordinates match the official planning layer metadata,
corroborating identity. Its field labelled positional accuracy contains `WGS_1984_UTM_Zone_47N`,
which is a CRS name, not a numerical accuracy. Source XY tolerance 0.001 m is storage precision.
Actual surveyed positional accuracy remains unknown; the 1 m guard remains an application assumption.
